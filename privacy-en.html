This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any third party.

Where your data lives

The coach name, client data, schedules, and days off you enter in the app are stored only on your own device (a local database). The developer has no access to it and no way to see it.

No account, no network

This app has no account system, requires no sign-up or login, has no analytics tools, no ads, and never connects to any server. The app runs fully offline.

The "Copy Schedule" feature

The "Copy" action in the client list copies that client's schedule as plain text to your phone's clipboard — you choose where to paste it (for example, a messaging app or a text message). The app itself never handles or records where you paste it.

Deleting your data

Removing the app deletes all local data on your device. No copy is kept anywhere else.

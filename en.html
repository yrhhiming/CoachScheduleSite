What kind of app is this?

CoachSchedule is a scheduling tool built for one coach to manage their own sessions — not a team scheduling system for multiple staff. No account system, no in-app purchases, no ads — all your data stays on your own phone, and the app runs fully offline.

🖐️ Drag to Plan
Long-press and drag a session to copy or move it in seconds
👥 Client Management
Search, numbered list, and one-tap copy of a client's upcoming schedule to share with them
✈️ Coach Days Off
Set a day off and the app blocks new sessions on it automatically
🔒 Fully Offline
Your data stays on your phone — no internet connection, no data uploads

User Guide

Follow the steps below — you'll be scheduling in a few minutes.

1First Launch

Open the app and the very first screen asks for your name — that's the app's one and only coach.

You can pick a language at the top (中文 / English) first; enter your name and tap Get Started to begin.

2This Week

After you submit your name you land on the Week screen — the main screen of the whole app, all seven days and every time slot laid out at a glance.

The round button in the top-left corner (☰) opens the menu, where you can switch between Week/Month view, manage clients, and set days off.

3Menu

The menu has three groups:

  • View: switch between Week and Month
  • Settings: Clients, Days Off, Language, Week Starts On
  • More: About, Feedback, Privacy Policy, Rate App

4Client Management

Tap “Clients” in the menu to manage all your clients.

★ The Scheduling Engine

5New Session, Move, Copy, Replace

This is the core of the app's scheduling logic, and the biggest difference from a normal calendar: one slot, one person — no overlapping sessions.

Drop it on a slot that's already taken: one slot, one person, never duplicated — you can only choose Replace or Cancel, so two sessions can never accidentally stack in the same slot.

6Coach Days Off

“Days Off” in the menu opens Month view and switches straight into day-off editing mode.

CoachSchedule is a fully offline personal tool — nothing is collected or uploaded.
